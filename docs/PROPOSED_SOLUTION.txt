8.  WHAT EACH STAGE IS ACTUALLY DOING
===============================================================================

Background for whoever presents the slide. This is NOT slide copy.

(1)  REACHES OUT
     Every channel terminates at one normalised ingestion interface: the
     victim app, the Integrated Portal, the existing chatbot, and 14566
     telephony through a licensed operator. The channel is recorded. No
     assessment has begun yet.

(2)  CONSENT FIRST
     State S0 plays a fixed, pre-recorded, human-approved script identifying
     the assistant as an AI, stating that a human officer reviews everything,
     and stating the right to a person at any time. Consent is stored as a
     verifiable artefact. A decline routes straight to a human queue and
     suppresses scoring entirely, rather than computing it and hiding it.

(3)  LISTENS
     State S1 takes the free narrative without interruption or probing. States
     S2 to S8 then ask only the licensed questions that the narrative left
     unanswered, and any state already answered is skipped. Target is eight to
     twelve turns total. A deterministic state machine chooses which question
     is asked; a language model is permitted only to phrase it, and every
     generated sentence is validated before it is spoken.

(4)  ASSESSES
     Nine dimensions are scored from the transcript and, on voice sessions,
     from prosody and speech emotion. Every dimension carries the identifiers
     of the turns it was derived from. This runs in parallel and never delays
     the reply.

(5)  PRIORITISES
     A 0 to 100 index across the nine weighted dimensions produces a band of
     Low, Moderate, High or Critical. Confirmed immediate danger or crisis
     forces Critical regardless of the weighted score. Where confidence is
     low, audio quality is poor, or the transcript cannot be trusted, the
     system returns Needs Human Assessment and produces no score at all.

(6)  HUMAN DECIDES
     The case enters an ordered queue that is never filtered. An executive
     claims it, reads the packet with its evidence links, and confirms,
     modifies, rejects, or takes over the live conversation. AI proposals and
     human decisions are stored in separate tables. Every action is written to
     an append-only audit log.

INTERRUPT BAND
     A synchronous, deterministic crisis pre-check runs ahead of dialogue
     policy on every single turn. A match forces state SX, a fixed
     counsellor-reviewed script, Critical priority and immediate human
     takeover, and intake never resumes on its own. No model makes this
     decision.

FOUNDATION BAR
     A server-side role filter removes every assessment field from anything a
     victim client receives: score, band, any of the nine dimensions, emotion
     label, confidence value, safety alert and officer notes. An automated
     test asserts it, and the build fails if the test fails.

9.7  WHAT GOES IN THE OTHER HALF
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The box is the mechanism. The other half should be the reason, not more
mechanism. In order of strength:

     1.  The victim voice. The existing quoted lines ("I'm scared", "I don't
         know what to do") with the "One conversation. No repetition." motif.
         This is the strongest pairing: feeling on one side, machinery on the
         other.

     2.  The friction list. Three or four cards of what happens today:
         repeated accounts, buried cues, inconsistent triage.

     3.  The outcome. Who benefits: victim, responder, district and ministry.

Do NOT put a second diagram beside it. Two diagrams on one slide compete and
neither is read.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     box title            16 pt bold
     strapline            10 pt

     2.  Place the header band and the foundation bar first. They are fixed.
     3.  Divide the remaining height by the number of stages and place
         invisible guides before drawing any card.

         top of the row dividers.

     Group the whole box as one object once built. It then moves between
     slides and between the two canvas sizes without falling apart.

slide contains anything rose, recolour that, not the rail.

10.8  PRACTICAL NOTE ON GENERATING VERSUS ASSEMBLING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Image generators still garble small text, and this slide is mostly small
text inside UI mockups. Two sane ways to use the prompt above:

     BEST      Generate only the pieces: the phone mockup, the fusion icon
               cluster, the hands illustration, the five feature icons.
               Then assemble the slide in PowerPoint and set every word of
               text as real text. Text stays editable, spell-checkable and
               sharp at any zoom, and the exported PDF keeps it selectable.

     FASTER    Generate the whole slide as a reference composition, then
               rebuild it in PowerPoint using the generated image only as a
               layout guide. Do not ship a generated slide with generated
               text on it: a misspelled word inside a picture cannot be
               fixed without regenerating, and judges do read the small text.

     NEVER     Ship the generated image directly. The current slide already
               contains one typo that survived into the built version; a
               generated one will contain several and they will be baked in.
